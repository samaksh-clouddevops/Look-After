# Play Console — Content rating (IARC) draft

Use the **IARC questionnaire** in Play Console. Suggested answers for Look After:

## App category
Utility / Productivity / Lifestyle

## Violence
- No realistic violence, no graphic violence: **No**

## Sexual content
- No sexual content, no nudity: **No**

## Language
- No profanity: **No** (coach copy is calm; user-generated notes possible but not encouraged)

## Controlled substances
- References to medications for personal tracking only: **Yes — informational / medical utility**
  - Not promoting recreational drug use
  - User-entered medication names/doses only

## Gambling
- No: **No**

## User interaction
- Users can communicate via optional body-double WebRTC room: **Yes — limited**
- Users share location: **No**
- Users share personal info publicly: **No**
- Users can interact online: **Optional** (Firebase / WebRTC when configured)

## Share location
- No

## Digital purchases
- No in-app products in 0.1.0: **No**

## Expected rating
**Everyone** / **PEGI 3** / **ESRB Everyone** (confirm after questionnaire).

## Age category notes
Not directed at children under 13. Medication tracking is adult self-care utility.
