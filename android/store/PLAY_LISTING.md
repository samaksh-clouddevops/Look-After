## Full description
Look After is a calm executive layer for noisy days.

**Today** — intentional board with sections, swipe actions, week scrubber, meds strip, and area/project filters.

**Briefing** — morning narrative from capacity, care, calendar, and optional cycle context.

**Brain** — offline coach plus optional cloud LLM. Multi-day horizon, streaming plans, mutation review, and pinned “why this hero” history.

**Focus** — timed ADHD sessions with ambient body double, optional camera mirror, DND when permitted, and multi-device room signaling.

**Care** — medications on the unified LifeEngine, Health Connect readiness and 7-day charts when available.

**Insights** — performance score, completion streaks, tag heat, board mix, and weekly review hints.

**Modules** — Travel packing, optional Cycle tracking, Learning spaced practice, Creativity boards, and Behavior habits.

Privacy-first and local by default. Optional Firebase Auth / sync when you add your own `google-services.json`. Optional OpenAI-compatible coach via `LOOKAFTER_LLM_API_KEY`.
